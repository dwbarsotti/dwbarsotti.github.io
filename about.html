Behind the Screen

Bridging the gap between
pedagogy and technology.

I am an Instructional Support Specialist in Distance Education currently working at Columbia College. I hold an M.S. in Instructional Design & Technology (MSIDT) alongside an M.Ed., combining strong academic foundations with hands-on technical execution.

My work revolves around ensuring that higher education institutions not only adopt new technologies, but implement them in ways that genuinely serve the cognitive and emotional needs of learners.

Design Philosophy

Robust Frameworks

Great learning doesn't happen by accident. I rely on proven instructional frameworks (like ADDIE and SAM) blended with cognitive load theory to architect experiences that stick.

Ethical AI in Higher Ed

As AI reshapes education, my focus is on integration that promotes academic integrity, mitigates algorithmic bias, and empowers rather than replaces the instructor.

Engaging Online Learning

Distance education shouldn't feel distant. I specialize in turning passive consumption into active, highly interactive environments that foster genuine connection.
