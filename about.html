Learner-centered Design

Developing cohesion between
pedagogy and technology.

Nothing is worse than a bad online learning experience. This fact led me to the field of online student support and educational technology. Through this journey, I've earned an M.S. in Instructional Design and Technology as well as an M.A. in Education, where I specialized in transforming dense academic material into accessible and engaging digital learning experiences.

As a lifelong learner myself, my passion thrives when removing the barriers from complex information, ultimately building environments that inspire the next generation of students to keep learning.

Design Philosophy

Designing Through the Eyes of a Student

This is the heartbeat of my design. Before a module is published, I navigate it as the end-user. If at any point I sense a roadblock for students, I immediately begin reiterating. I believe that empathy is the most important tool in my technological stack and is the waystone for my learner-centered design.

Student Access

Learning cannot happen if the knowledge is locked behind a barrier. I prioritize universal design and strict accessibility standards to ensure that every learner has frictionless access to course materials.

Engaging Frameworks

A beautiful course is great, but a beautiful and effective course is better. I utilize proven instructional frameworks to build logical, intuitive modules that guide learners step-by-step, minimizing cognitive overload and maximizing engagement.

Learner Autonomy

My goal is to build environments where adult learners take ownership of their journey. By designing clear navigation and transparent objectives, I empower students to become active, self-directed explorers rather than passive consumers.

Ethical AI in Education

As artificial intelligence reshapes higher education, I champion its integration with integrity. I focus on developing policies and curriculums that utilize AI as a tool for equity and empowerment, enhancing human learning rather than replacing it.
